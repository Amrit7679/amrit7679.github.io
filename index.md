## ✅ How to Use pocket-reader
Using pocket-reader is easy. Here’s how:

1. **Open your favorite browser.** 

2. **Select the pocket-reader icon** in your toolbar.

3. **Navigate to the webpage** you want to be read aloud.

4. Click on the pocket-reader icon again and select **"Read Aloud"** from the menu that appears.

5. Adjust the reading speed and voice settings through the options menu if desired.

## 💡 Features
- Reads text from any webpage.
- Offers adjustable reading speed.
- Provides a selection of voices depending on your OS.
- Highlights text as it's read aloud for easy following.

## 🤔 Troubleshooting
If you encounter issues while using pocket-reader, consider the following steps:

1. **Check if the extension is enabled** in your browser’s settings.

3. **Restart your browser** if the extension fails to respond.

4. If problems persist, **reinstall the extension** by removing it and following the download instructions again.
